What this is. Peter’s Design V3.2 drawn to scale off his 1 m grid, on the business-model-2 decisions of 28 Aug: the whole ground floor is our event floor, the deck carries four 4 × 5 m studios behind 3 m curtains plus our 4 × 6 m office, and the Wood & Build Area is Peter’s own. Two layers of curtain: each studio has its own, and a second run hangs just inside the rail along the whole open edge — walkway, office, kitchen — so that closed (tick Event mode), the upstairs disappears from an event and tenants come and go unseen; the rail and the plants stay on the event side. Nothing is shared and nothing is lockable — a curtain is a curtain.

* Peter writes 4 m below and 4 m above, which leaves nothing for the floor itself. Drawn here: 4.00 m clear, a 0.28 m steel floor (assumed, not quoted), ~3.7 m above. The deck is steel because V3.2 shows a steel example; nothing is chosen.

The stair is drawn at a legal pitch — 23 risers of 186 mm, 4.84 m of run — where Peter’s drawing gives it ~3.5 m; his example render has two stairs, his plan one. Divider-wall height, doors and every piece of furniture are illustrative, not in the brief. No bar and no projection wall appear in V3.2, so none are drawn.
