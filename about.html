About us

We kept looking for a space like this. So we are building it.

We are Helena Dolabela and Peter Bout. We run About Collab, a creative studio running workshops for teams in Amsterdam and across the Netherlands.

We have our own space at Het Salon, and we love it. But the moment a group is too big for it, or the client is somewhere else in the country, we are booking someone else’s room — and that is where this came from.

Why we are doing this

Renting a room should not be this hard.

Booking a venue in the Netherlands means emailing ten places, waiting days for a reply, and being sent a PDF with prices you could not have guessed. Half of them never answer at all. We have done it in Amsterdam, in The Hague, and everywhere in between — and we are doing it again this week.

Every time, the same thought: this is a good business being run badly. The rooms are fine. The way you are made to book them is not.

So we are building the one we wanted.

A space where the prices are simply published, where you can work out what your event costs without asking anyone, and where a person answers you within a day.

It has to work for our own workshops first — we will be using it every week. But the same things that make it good for us make it good for anyone booking a dinner, a borrel or an offsite: clear prices, an easy yes, and a room with some character in it.

What we are actually good at

Designing the whole experience, not renting a box.

What we enjoy — and what About Collab has been paid to do for years — is designing an experience end to end: the room, the food, the drinks, and something for people to make together.

Doing that inside our own building, with our own kitchen and our own floor, is simply the complete version of the thing we were already doing in other people's rooms. That is the whole idea behind Studio About.
