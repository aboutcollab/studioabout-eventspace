What you can host

Most of what a room can be for.

Every one of these opens the calculator already filled in, so you can see a real number in about five seconds and then change it to match your own event.

Business

Offsites, training and the serious days

Strategy days, team offsites, training and courses, board and team meetings, sprints and hackathons, launches and press moments, networking, seminars, and brands taking the room for a few days to sell or show something.

The room, coffee and tea from the moment you arrive, lunch, and a borrel at the end if the day earns one. Projector and screen included.

Price it: Workshop for 24, 09:00–17:00, buffet lunch

Celebration

Borrels, team outings and parties

Company borrels, Friday drinks, team outings, kerstborrel and summer drinks, birthdays, jubilees, farewells, graduations, and kids' parties.

Standing room, the bar open, bites coming out of the kitchen, and the music as loud as you need it. Hours after 20:00 carry the night rate, and the night can run to 04:00.

Price it: Borrel for 60, 17:00–23:00, drinks and bites

Dinner

Long tables and a proper meal

Team dinners, client dinners, buffets and walking dinners, supper clubs, and the churrasco: an amazing Brazilian barbecue dinner, carved at the table.

Everyone seated, the kitchen forty steps away, and wine on the table priced by the hour instead of by the bottle.

Price it: Dinner for 40, 18:00–23:00, buffet and wine

Creative

Workshops, classes and shows

Creative workshops and classes, exhibitions and openings, maker demonstrations and masterclasses, run by you, by About Collab, or by one of the makers upstairs.

A room you are allowed to make a mess in, in a building where that is normal. Tables, water, and floors that have seen worse.

Price it: Workshop for 16, 13:00–18:00, simple lunch
