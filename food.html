Sabô de Aya · our kitchen

The food is an experience in itself.

Sabô de Aya cook Brazilian comfort food, full of flavour and built around your event, from a simple hot lunch to a churrasco carved at the table. The part of the evening people remember.

Book a room with food

Our catering partner

Sabô de Aya.

Maeli and Keyla are Brazilian chefs, cooking comfort food you will not find on another venue’s menu: moqueca de banana da terra, frango in peanut sauce with vatápá, coxinhas and warm pão de queijo, a passion-fruit lava cake to finish.

There are plenty of options and every menu is built around your event: lunch or dinner, a buffet or served at the table, one hot dish or a full churrasco carved in front of everyone. Vegan throughout, and they cook around whatever your group cannot, or would rather not, eat.

This is speciality cooking at the price a venue normally charges for ordinary catering. Lunch starts at €20 a person and dinner at €29,50. And it is the part of the evening people are still talking about on the way home.
