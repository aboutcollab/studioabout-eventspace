How many of you are there?

That is the only question that decides the price. Pick the size that fits your group. The room is closed down to it, so a workshop for sixteen sits in a room built for sixteen, not in the corner of a hall.

It is one room. Curtains on ceiling tracks close it to the size you booked, and open again for the next event. That is why one space can hold a workshop for sixteen on Tuesday and a hundred people standing on Saturday, and why you only pay for the part you use.

What an hour costs

Pay for the size you use.

The room is priced in three sizes, one price every day of the week. Set-up and clear-out sit inside the window you book; overtime is charged at the same hourly rate, never a penalty rate. A projector and screen come with the room.
