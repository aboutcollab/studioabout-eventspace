With About Collab

Give them something to make.

A room and a lunch is a fine day out. A room, a lunch and three hours of making something together is the one people talk about afterwards.

About Collab is our own creative studio. They have run workshops for teams across the Netherlands for years, and they work in this building.

How it fits together

One day, one invoice.

The room

Priced by the hour on this site, from €50. Set up however the day needs it.

The food

Lunch, dinner or a borrel from our own kitchen, priced per person.

The workshop

Designed and run by About Collab. Priced per person, depending on what you want to make.

Most venues can give you the first two. The third is why a team remembers the day — and it is the reason we keep the room deliberately cheap.

See the workshops at About Collab Price the room and the food

Workshop pricing lives on the About Collab site, where it belongs — every workshop is designed for the group, so it is quoted rather than listed.

And the people upstairs

A building full of people who make things.

Above the event floor are studios, and in them are makers — ceramicists, designers, people who run their own classes. Some of them will run something for your group too. If what you want is not on our list, ask: there is a good chance somebody forty steps away does exactly that.
