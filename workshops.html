With About Collab

Team bonding through art.

Make a piece of art together, and take it back to the office.

About Collab is our own creative studio, run by the same two people in the same building. We run creative team-building workshops for teams, events and whole organisations, from a relaxed creative afternoon to a deep team insight session.

Groups of 4 to 2,000 No art experience needed In this building, or anywhere in the Netherlands

The workshops

Four ways in.

Every one is built around your group, your theme and your goals. No art experience is needed, ever, by anyone.

The Art of Values

Bring your company’s core values to life through collaborative art. Enhance your bond and create lasting, tangible memories of your shared principles.

The Art of Personalities

Enhance team dynamics and communication by creatively exploring personality differences. Foster genuine connections and mutual appreciation through collaborative artistic expression.

The Art of Play

Our most playful and impactful workshop. Your team works together through guided creative exercises and fun art games to build a collective artwork. And you help children play.

The Art of Creativity

Unleash creativity with fully customised artistic experiences tailored to your event or strategic goals. Foster collaboration and celebrate milestones as we create memorable artworks together.

The collective artwork

Everyone makes a piece. It becomes one thing.

Every About Collab workshop ends in a single artwork the group made together. Under guidance, your team designs and builds something that goes back with you and keeps working long after the day itself.

It is the part that makes a workshop different from an activity. A year later it is still on the wall, and people still point at the bit they made.

How it goes

Four steps, and we do most of them.

1

Greeting & briefing

A friendly, no-commitment ten-minute call so we understand what the day is for.

2

Idea & concept

We come back with workshop concepts made for your team and your event. You pick one.

3

Production & preparation

We design and prepare every material, tool and piece of kit. You do nothing.

4

Let’s make art together

The workshop itself, in this building, with the room and the food already sorted.

Why it belongs here

A room, a meal, and something to actually do.

Most places rent you a room and leave you to it. Here you can book the space, the food and the workshop as one day, in one building, from one page.

Workshop pricing lives on the About Collab site, where it belongs. Every workshop is designed for the group, so it is quoted rather than listed.
